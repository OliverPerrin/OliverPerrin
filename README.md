### Research on the side

[**LexiMind**](https://github.com/OliverPerrin/LexiMind) began as my independent undergraduate research project. It includes a transformer implemented from scratch in PyTorch, with FLAN-T5 weight transfer and a shared encoder for summarisation, emotion classification, and topic classification. My current interests include shared representations, adaptation, and how to use a fixed post-training compute budget well.

The repository also contains a [book-discovery app](https://leximind-five.vercel.app). Its content-based recommendations run separately from the research model. Ongoing experiments and their limitations are documented in the [research notes](https://github.com/OliverPerrin/LexiMind/blob/main/docs/research/README.md).
